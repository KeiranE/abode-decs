Commercial Property Maintenance

Painting, decorating and property maintenance for commercial spaces, with the work planned around the premises and agreed scope.

Request a quote →

What’s included

The right work for your space.

  • Commercial painting & decorating
  • Interior & exterior finishes
  • Property repairs & improvements within scope
  • Larger projects considered further afield

A practical mix of services

Discuss decorating, spraying, flooring, joinery, tiling or plumbing requirements as appropriate to the premises. Regulated gas and electrical work remain contractor services.

Commercial coverage

Commercial projects are normally considered within around 25 miles of Leicester. Larger jobs may be considered up to around 50 miles; send the location and scope to check.

Planning the work

Explain access arrangements, any operating hours and the timescale you have in mind. Scheduling and restrictions are agreed for the job; no out-of-hours service is promised.

Common questions

Good to know.

Can you cover several types of work?

Yes, where the work falls within the services offered. Include all requirements in your enquiry so the scope can be assessed.

Do you travel for larger commercial jobs?

Larger projects may be considered up to around 50 miles from Leicester. Share the address and project details.

Do you offer an emergency or ongoing contract service?

Contact us to discuss your requirements. Emergency availability or a maintenance contract is not included as a standard promise.
